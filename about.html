About SkillBridge

We're on a mission to make early-career hiring transparent, fast and fair for every student in India.

Our story

SkillBridge started as a college project to fix a frustrating problem — students applying to dozens of internships through scattered Google Forms and never hearing back. Today, we partner with 300+ companies to bring structure, accountability and delight to early-career hiring.

Built for the Smart India Hackathon, we believe a great product can do more than connect resumes to recruiters — it can shape careers.
